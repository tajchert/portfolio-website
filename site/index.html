I'm the person who looks at your microwave and wonders if it can run Doom. Most often, it can.

I'm a builder first. I started as an Android developer, ran three startups, and led mobile teams in banking and fashion e-commerce. Today I'm CTO at Lite e-Commerce, leading software, design and data for Żabka Jush and Delio, and I still write code most weeks.

I like small, fast teams with startup habits: ship early, own the result, fix what breaks. The people I most enjoy working with are ambitious and happy to take on something they have never done before.

I learn by building. Kanarek.app, my air-quality app for Poland, has more than 200k active users and a 4.8 rating, and the apps, CLIs and MCP tools below are how I try new technology first-hand. I won 1st prize at Warsaw Hacks, organized GDG Warszawa and have spoken at many Android events.
